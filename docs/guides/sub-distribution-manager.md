# Sub Distribution MD / Manager Guide

As **Sub Distribution MD / Manager** you run a field region: you manage the sub-distribution
team, view the devices and distributions in your scope, review field operations for approval,
and manage replacements, returns, and pending dues. Your dashboard is the
**Sub Distributor Dashboard** (scoped to your region).

Read **[getting-started.md](getting-started.md)** first for login and the shared interface.

## Your dashboard

The scoped dashboard shows your region's KPIs — devices, distributions, return requests,
and defects — for the team you manage.

## User management (scoped)

- **All Users** — list the users in your hierarchy. You can **create** users in your team,
  edit them, and manage their roles/status within your scope.
- **Bulk Upload Users** — upload a CSV of users for your team at once.

## Devices

- **My Devices** — the devices in your region. View details and history.
- **Track Device** — view a device's location and status chain by serial.

## Distribution

- **Scoped Distributions** — distributions for your region and their status.
- **Distribution** — create new distributions to hand devices to your team (sub
  distributors, clusters, operators).

## Approvals — your key responsibility

You are an **approval reviewer** for your field team:

- **Sub Distribution Approvals** — approve or reject **sub distribution** requests raised
  by your team.
- **Operations Approvals** — approve or reject field **operations** requests (such as
  cluster actions and returns).

These two pages are where your team's field requests land. Review the details and approve
or reject with the on-screen buttons.

## Defects, Replacements, Returns & Dues

- **Defect Reports** — view and manage defect reports in your region; update and resolve.
- **Replacements** — view replacement items (shown when there are received returns at the
  office). Manage the replacement workflow.
- **Pending Payments** — view and manage payment dues in your region.
- **Returns** — view and manage return records.

## Reports (scoped)

- **Cluster Report** and **Operator Report** — scoped reporting views for your team.
- **Report** — your scoped reporting overview.

## External Inventory

- **External Inventory** — view external inventory items relevant to your region.

## Features summary

"You can" — manage your team's users, view/manage scoped devices and distributions, create
distributions, **approve sub-distribution and operations requests**, manage defects,
replacements, returns, and dues, and view scoped reports.

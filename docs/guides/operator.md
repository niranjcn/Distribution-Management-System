# Operator Guide

As **Operator** you work directly with devices in the field: you manage your own devices
and distributions, report defects, confirm deliveries and replacement receipt, and view
your returns and pending payments. Your dashboard is the **Operator Dashboard** (your
personal stats).

Read **[getting-started.md](getting-started.md)** first for login and the shared interface.

## Your dashboard

The Operator Dashboard shows **your personal** numbers: devices, distributions, defects,
and returns that involve you, plus your chart analytics. This is your day-to-day home.

## Devices

- **My Devices** — the devices assigned to you. View details and history.
- **Track Device** — view any device's location and status chain by serial.
- **Device edits** — if a device record is wrong, request an edit via your management.

## Distribution

- **Distribution** — create distributions where you hand a device to someone (or receive
  it). 
- **Delivery Confirmations** — confirm receipt of distributions delivered to you. When a
  device arrives, confirm it here so the record stays accurate.

## Defects

- **My Defect Reports** — the defect reports you submitted and their status.
- **Report Defect** — report a defective device:
  1. Open **Report Defect**.
  2. Search for and select the defective device.
  3. Describe the problem and attach a photo if available.
  4. Submit. The report goes to your management for action.

## Replacements & Confirmations

- **Replacements** — replacement items (shown when there are received returns at the
  office). View the status of your replacement items.
- **Replacement Confirmation** — confirm receipt of a replacement device when it arrives.

## Returns & Dues

- **Returns** — your return records; submit returns for defective/excess devices and view
  their status.
- **Pending Payments** — payment dues involving you.

## External Inventory

- **External Inventory** — view external inventory items relevant to you.

## Features summary

"You can" — manage your own devices and distributions, create distributions, confirm
deliveries and replacements, report defects and track their status, view your returns and
dues, and view relevant external inventory. Keep field records current — confirm every
delivery and report every defective device promptly.

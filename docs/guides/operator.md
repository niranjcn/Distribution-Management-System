# Operator Guide

**Who you are:** As **Operator** you hold and use the devices assigned to you under one
cluster in the field. You confirm every delivery that arrives to you, distribute devices
between fellow operators when needed, report defects on your devices, confirm
replacement receipts, and watch the payment dues tied to your returned devices. Your
workspace is the **Operator Dashboard**, scoped to your own devices.

You are the last link in the chain **PDIC → Sub Distributor → Cluster → Operator**:
devices reach you through your cluster, and once you confirm a delivery, the devices are
yours. Prompt confirmations keep the whole chain moving, so confirm what you actually
receive.

Read **[getting-started.md](getting-started.md)** first for the login basics and the
layout (sidebar on the left, notification bell and profile menu top-right).

## 1. Managing your profile & credentials

**Purpose:** keep your own account accurate — your details, email, phone, and password
are all managed from your **Profile** page. When you log in for the first time (or when
your cluster/sub-distributor marks your account for an update), you are asked to set a new
email and/or password before entering the site — do that once and then continue.

### Step-by-step: change your details later (Profile)

1. Click the **profile menu** in the **top-right corner** of the screen.
2. Click **Profile** from the dropdown.
3. You will see two tabs at the top of the page: **Profile Information** and **Security**.

**Edit your profile details (name, designation, address, pincode):**

1. Stay on the **Profile Information** tab (or click it if you are on Security).
2. Edit the **Full Name**, **Designation**, **Address**, and **Pincode** fields.
3. Click **Save Changes** at the bottom. A green "Profile updated successfully" message
   confirms it.

### Change your email or phone

1. Click the **Security** tab.
2. In the **Email Address** card, see your **Current Email** (greyed out) and type the
   **New Email** in the box, then click **Change Email**.
3. In the **Phone Number** card, type the new number (min 10 digits) in the
   **New Phone Number** box, then click **Change Phone**.

### Change your password

1. On the **Security** tab, find the **Password** card.
2. Enter **Current Password** (your existing password).
3. Enter **New Password** (at least 6 characters) and **Confirm New Password**.
4. Click **Update Password**. A green message confirms it.

> **Want a different credential changed?** Your profile lets you change your own details
> any time. If your account ever needs a credential change outside your scope, contact
> your cluster / sub-distributor so the management team can apply it.

## 2. Your dashboard

Your **Operator Dashboard** is the first screen after login and gives you a real-time
pulse of *your own* work: the devices assigned to you, their active/in-use status, and
your defect reports.

Use the **date range filter** (top right) to focus the numbers — Today, Last 7/30/60/90
days, This Year, All Time, or a **Custom Range**. Setting it first makes every number
below match the period you care about.

### The headline numbers

Four cards summarise your account:

- **Assigned Devices** — how many devices are assigned to you.
- **Active** — your devices that are active.
- **In Use** — your devices currently in use.
- **My Defect Reports** — how many defect reports you have filed.

A **doughnut chart** shows your **My Device Active vs Inactive** split at a glance.

### The pending receipt banner

If any delivery is waiting for your confirmation, an orange banner appears at the top:
*"You have N delivery(ies) to confirm — confirm that you have received the devices before
you can redistribute them"*, with a **Confirm Now** button that jumps straight to
**Delivery Confirmations** (section 8).

### My Assigned Devices

A grid of your devices, each showing its **status**, its **identity** (NUID / MAC /
serial), and two actions:

- **View** — opens the device's **Track Device** journey.
- **Report** — jumps to the defect-report form for that device.

**View all** opens the **My Devices** page (section 4).

### Defect & returns cards

Two side-by-side cards:

- **My Defect Reports** — your defect reports with severity and status; **View all**
  opens **Defect Reports**, and **Report a defect** links to the report form.
- **My Return Requests** — your return records with their status; **View all** opens
  **My Returns** (section 10).

## 3. Finding a device (Track Device)

**Purpose:** the **Track Device** page is where you look up any device and see exactly
where it is and everywhere it has been. It appears in your sidebar.

### Step-by-step: search for a specific device

1. Open **Track Device** from the sidebar.
2. In the search box at the top, type the device's **serial number**, **NUID**, or
   **MAC address**.
3. Click **Track Device** (or press Enter).

### What you see in the result

- **Device Info card** — manufacturer, MAC, serial, device type, and device ID.
- **Current Location** — who holds the device now (for example "Operator — Ram Kumar").
  Colours help: Sub Distributor (purple), Cluster (indigo), Operator (green).
- **Availability Status** — the current status (available, distributed, in use,
  defective, returned, etc.).
- **Device Journey** — a timeline of every action on the device (registered, distributed
  person A → person B, status changes). The most recent entry is marked **current**.
- **Distribution Flow** — the chain **PDIC → Sub Distributor → Cluster → Operator** with
  the current step highlighted.

## 4. Devices (My Devices)

**Purpose:** **My Devices** shows your own work device list — what is assigned to you.

1. Open **My Devices** from the sidebar.
2. The summary cards at top show your personal totals: **In My Hand**, **Total in
   Chain**, **Total Received**, **Total Sent**, and **Transfers** (distributions
   received/out).
3. **Search Devices** with filters by type, manufacturer, status, or holder to find a
   specific device.
4. Click a row (or the **eye** icon) to open **Device Details** — status, holder,
   location, and the full field list. From there you can **Track Device** to see the
   journey (section 3).

> **Why this matters:** this is your inventory view. Before creating or receiving a
> distribution, check here that the devices are where you expect them.

## 5. External Inventory

All external inventory records belong to **PDIC management** — you get a **view-only**
catalog of the items. Open **External Inventory** from the sidebar and you see the
**Items** tab: each row shows the **ID, Name, and Price** of the fixture (OTT boxes,
OLTs, remotes, SB/adapters, and other stocked items). You can search and filter, but you
cannot add, edit, or distribute them — that's management's job.

## 6. The distribution workflow

**Purpose:** distributions are how devices move down the chain —
**PDIC → Sub Distributor → Cluster → Operator**. As an operator you are mostly a
**receiver**: devices come to you after your cluster confirms them. Rarely (between
operators in the same cluster) you can also **send** a device onward.

### 6.1 How devices arrive to you

1. **Your cluster sends you a distribution** — the distribution starts in
   **Awaiting Receipt** (`pending_receipt`).
2. **You are notified** — a warning notification ("Action Required: Confirm Device
   Receipt") points you to the **Delivery Confirmations** page.
3. **You confirm or dispute**:
   - **Confirm Received** → the distribution becomes **Confirmed** and the devices are
     yours to use.
   - **Not Received** → the distribution becomes **Disputed**; management and the sender
     are alerted immediately.

> **The golden rule:** devices stay with the sender until the recipient confirms receipt.
> Only after confirmation do the devices move into your account.

### 6.2 Your part in the workflow

- **As receiver:** confirm every delivery that arrives to you promptly — this unlocks
  the devices for your use.
- **As sender (rare):** if you *do* transfer devices onward, distribute to one of the
  **operators in your own cluster**; they then confirm receipt, and the device is theirs.
- **Disputed returns are resolved by management** — when a delivery to you is disputed
  and devices are still with the sender, management coordinates the outcome; confirm
  receipt promptly so disputes are rare.

### 6.3 The statuses, explained

| Status | What it means | Who acts |
|--------|---------------|----------|
| **Awaiting Receipt** (`pending_receipt`) | Sent; the receiver hasn't confirmed yet. Devices are still effectively the sender's. | Receiver confirms or disputes |
| **Confirmed** (`approved`) | Receiver confirmed receipt. Devices now belong to them. | Receiver (already done) |
| **Disputed** (`disputed`) | Receiver says the devices did **not** arrive. Sender and management are notified. | Recipient / management |

## 7. Managing distributions

**Purpose:** create and track distributions from your account. You have **My
Distributions**, **Create Distribution**, and **Bulk Upload** under the **Distribution**
menu.

### 7.1 Step-by-step: distribute to another operator

1. Open **Distribution → Create Distribution** from the sidebar.
2. On the left, **select the devices** to send (manual selection or **Select Filtered
   (N)** after applying a filter such as status).
3. **Select Recipient:** pick the **Operator** who is to receive it. The list contains
   the other operators in your cluster (you are excluded automatically).
4. Set the **Date of Distribution** (leave blank for today) and add any notes.
5. Click **Create Distribution**, review, and **Confirm Transfer**.

### 7.2 Step-by-step: bulk upload a distribution

1. Open **Distribution → Bulk Upload**.
2. **Download the CSV template** — one row per device (`mac_address`, `serial_number`,
   `nuid`).
3. Fill it with your devices and **select the recipient** (another operator in your
   cluster).
4. Review the result summary, fix any error rows, and re-upload.

### 7.3 Step-by-step: view and analyse your distributions

1. Open **Distribution → My Distributions**. The cards give a quick snapshot: **Total,
   Awaiting Receipt, Confirmed, Disputed**.
2. Set the **date range** to focus on a period.
3. **Search** by all fields, distribution ID, from/to, status, confirmed-by, or date.
4. Click a row to open **Distribution Details** — the sender, receiver, device summary,
   and status.

### 7.4 Downloading the device list

In the **Distribution details**, click **Download CSV / Excel** to export exactly which
devices (MAC / NUID) were sent in that batch. For deliveries **you receive**, this
download is available to you directly and is handy for checking a shipment against what
arrived.

## 8. Delivery Confirmations

**Purpose:** this page is where every delivery **to you** gets confirmed or disputed. It
is the moment a transfer becomes final.

### 8.1 Step-by-step: confirm or dispute a delivery

1. Open **Delivery Confirmations** from the sidebar (or use the banner on your
   dashboard).
2. The list shows each distribution that is **Awaiting Confirmation**:
   - **Confirm Received** — the devices transfer into your account.
   - **Not Received** — you did **not** receive it (this raises a dispute). Sender and
     management are alerted.
3. Add notes if useful, then submit.

> **Why it matters:** a device that isn't confirmed stays with the sender and never
> reaches your account. Confirm every delivery that physically arrives, as soon as it
> arrives.

## 9. Defect Reports

**Purpose:** when one of your devices fails, the cycle is: **you report** the defect →
management **reviews** it → the device is **returned** → a **replacement is assigned** →
**you confirm** the replacement physically arrives.

### 9.1 The defect workflow (read this first)

1. **You report a defect** — select the faulty device, describe the issue, add a photo
   if available, and submit.
2. **Management reviews** — and approves or rejects:
   - **Approve** → a **return request is automatically created** and you are told to
     return the faulty device.
   - **Reject** → the report is closed.
3. **The device comes back** — management confirms it reached PDIC. Only then is a
   replacement possible.
4. **A replacement is assigned** — the defect moves to
   `replacement_pending_confirmation`.
5. **You confirm the replacement** — once the replacement device physically arrives, the
   report becomes **resolved** and the device activates in your account.

### 9.2 Step-by-step: report a defect

You can start from anywhere:

- Open **Defects → Report Defect** in the sidebar, **or**
- Click the **Report Defect** button on your dashboard, **or**
- Use **Report** on a device card in your dashboard's **My Assigned Devices**.

Then:

1. **Select the device** — search by model, serial, or NUID (or it is pre-filled if you
   started from a device).
2. Fill in the **Defect Type**, **Severity**, a **Description**, and attach a **photo**
   if available.
3. Submit. The device is marked defective and management is notified to review it.

### 9.3 Step-by-step: confirm a replacement receipt

1. Open **My Defect Reports** (sidebar → **Defect** → **My Defect Reports**).
2. A report in `replacement_pending_confirmation` shows a green **Confirm** action.
3. **Only after the replacement device physically arrives**, click **Confirm**.
4. The defect becomes **resolved** and the replacement is **activated in your account**
   (a "Replacement Receipt Confirmed" notification appears in **Notifications**).

> **The golden rule:** confirm only after physically receiving the device. Confirming
> early would mark the replacement active before it actually reached you.

### 9.4 The Defect Reports page, in detail

- **Summary cards** — Total, Open, Under Review, Resolved, Critical (for your reports).
- **Search** — by report ID, serial, NUID, description, type, severity, status, etc.
- **Table columns** — device, type, severity, reported by, date, status, and **payment
  status**.
- **Row actions** — the **eye** opens full details; the **Confirm** action appears on
  replacements waiting for your receipt.

## 10. Returns

**Purpose:** the **Returns** page shows your **return requests** — the records created
for your defective devices that were approved for return.

1. Open **My Returns** from the sidebar.
2. See each return request: the device, the reason, and its **status**.
3. Return confirmation (marking a device as received at PDIC) is handled by
   management/PDIC — your part is to ensure the device physically goes back once your
   return request is created.

## 11. Pending Payments & Replacements

### 11.1 The Pending Payments page

Your **Pending Payments** page is scoped to **your own dues** — amounts owed for returned
defective devices that have been received at PDIC, have a return amount above zero, and
are not yet confirmed.

1. Open **Pending Payments** from the sidebar.
2. Summary cards show **Pending Items** and **Outstanding Amount**.
3. The **Due Details** list shows each pending item: report ID, device model/NUID,
   return ID, return status, **Received At PDIC** date, and **Due Amount**.
4. If a **Payment Bill** is available on an item, use **View Bill** to open it.

> **Your action:** this is how you know what you currently owe. Payment confirmation is
> handled by PDIC/Management — your part is to view and keep track.

### 11.2 The Replacements pages

**Purpose:** replacement records link each **defective device → replacement device**
mapping. The **Replacements** menu appears when there are **received returns**.

1. **Replacements** — the list of defect→replacement mappings; search and open rows.
2. **Pending Replacements** — approved defects still awaiting a replacement device, split
   into **awaiting / ready / waiting** counts.

### 11.3 Replacement Confirmation

When a replacement has been assigned to you and is ready, the **Replacement Confirmation**
page (and the **Confirm** action on the Defect Reports page) is where you complete the
hand-off:

1. Open **Replacement Confirmation** from the sidebar (it shows when there is replacement
   work for you).
2. See the replacement waiting for your confirmation.
3. Confirm **only after the device physically arrives**. The replacement becomes active in
   your account and the defect is resolved.

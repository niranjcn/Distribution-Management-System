# Sub Distributor Guide

As **Sub Distributor** you run distribution operations in the field: you receive and
distribute devices, confirm deliveries, manage defects and returns, handle replacements
and pending payments, and act as an **approval reviewer** for sub-distribution and field
operations. Your dashboard is the **Sub Distributor Dashboard** (scoped to your area).

Read **[getting-started.md](getting-started.md)** first for login and the shared interface.

## Your dashboard

The scoped dashboard shows your area's KPIs: devices, distributions, return requests,
and defects.

## Devices

- **My Devices** — the devices in your area. View details and history.
- **Track Device** — view a device's location and status chain by serial.

## Distribution

- **Distribution** — create distributions to hand devices down to your team.
- **Scoped Distributions** — view the distributions in your area and their status.
- **Delivery Confirmations** — confirm receipt of distributions delivered to you or your
  area. When a delivery arrives, confirm it here so the record is complete.

## Approvals — your key responsibility

You are an **approval reviewer** for field operations:

- **Sub Distribution Approvals** — approve or reject **sub distribution** requests in your
  area.
- **Operations Approvals** — approve or reject field **operations** requests (cluster
  actions, returns, etc.).

Review the request details, then approve or reject with the on-screen buttons.

## Defects, Replacements, Returns & Dues

- **Defect Reports** — view and manage defect reports; create new reports when you find a
  defective device; update and resolve.
- **Replacements** — view replacement items (menu shows them when there are received
  returns at the office). Manage the replacement workflow.
- **Replacement Confirmation** — confirm receipt of a replacement device when it arrives.
- **Pending Payments** — view and manage payment dues.
- **Returns** — submit return records for defective/excess devices and manage their status.

## User management (scoped)

- **All Users** — list users in your scope; you can create users in your team and manage
  their roles/status within your area.
- **Bulk Upload Users** — upload a CSV of users for your area.

## Reports (scoped)

- **Cluster Report** and **Operator Report** — scoped reporting views for your area.
- **Report** — your scoped reporting overview.

## External Inventory

- **External Inventory** — view external inventory items relevant to your area.

## Features summary

"You can" — create distributions and confirm deliveries, manage defects, replacements,
returns, and dues, submit returns, approve sub-distribution and operations requests,
manage your team's users, and view scoped reports and external inventory.

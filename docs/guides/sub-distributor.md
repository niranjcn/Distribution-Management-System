# Sub Distributor Guide

As **Sub Distributor** you run distribution operations in the field: you receive and
distribute devices, confirm deliveries, report defects and confirm replacements, handle
returns and pending payments, and grow your team by creating users under your
sub-distribution. Your dashboard is the **Sub Distributor Dashboard** (scoped to your
area and the clusters/operators under you).

Read **[getting-started.md](getting-started.md)** first for login and the shared
interface.

## Your dashboard

Your scoped dashboard gives you a real-time picture of your area:

- **Stat cards** — Received Devices, Pending Confirmations, My Operators, Defect
  Reports, Returns, and Distributions sent.
- **Devices Under My Chain** — Total Devices, Total Devices In Hand, and devices held
  by the clusters/operators under you.
- **Charts** — your devices by Active/Inactive, and your cluster/operator accounts by
  Active/Inactive.
- **Pending receipt banner** — if any deliveries are waiting for your confirmation, an
  orange banner appears with a **Confirm Now** button that jumps straight to
  **Delivery Confirmations**.
- **Cards** — your recent devices, pending confirmations, operators, defect reports to
  review, and return requests.

From the dashboard you can also click **Assign to Operator** to start a new distribution.

## Track Device

Use **Track Device** to look up any device by **serial number**, **NUID**, or **MAC
address** and see:

- The **Device Info** card and its **Current Location** (who holds it now — PDIC,
  Sub Distributor, Cluster, or Operator).
- Its **Availability Status** (available, distributed, in use, defective, returned, …).
- Its full **Device Journey** timeline and the **Distribution Flow** chain
  (**PDIC → Sub Distributor → Cluster → Operator**) with the current step highlighted.

## Devices (My Devices)

**My Devices** shows the devices in your area, scoped to your sub-distribution: what you
hold plus what your clusters and operators hold. Open any device to view its details and
full history.

## Distribution

You are the link that moves devices from headquarters down to the field:

- **Create Distribution** — send devices you hold to a **Cluster** or **Operator**
  directly under your sub-distribution. Pick the recipient type, then the specific
  cluster or operator, select the devices, and confirm the transfer.
- **Bulk Upload** — create a distribution from a CSV/Excel file of
  `mac_address, serial_number, nuid` rows for a single recipient.
- **My Distributions** — see the distributions you have sent or received and their
  status (Awaiting Receipt, Confirmed, Disputed).
- **Delivery Confirmations** — every time a distribution is delivered **to you**, you
  confirm or dispute receipt here. **Confirm** the devices physically arrived so the
  transfer becomes final and the devices unlock for redistribution; **Dispute** if the
  delivery did not arrive. You can also confirm receipt from the Distribution details
  page, and download the distribution's **MAC/NUID list** for the deliveries you receive.

> **The golden rule:** devices stay with the sender until you confirm receipt. Confirming
> is what makes the hand-off final, so confirm every delivery that arrives.

## Defects

- **Report a Defect** — when you find a defective device, create a **Defect Report**
  from the Defect Reports page: select the device, describe the issue, add a photo if
  available, and submit. The device is marked defective and management is alerted to
  review it.
- **Confirm Replacement** — when a replacement device is assigned to you, the report
  shows a **Confirm** action once it physically arrives. Confirming activates the
  replacement in your account and resolves the defect.
- **Defect Reports** — view the reports in your area and their status, including the
  ones reported by your clusters and operators.

## Replacements, Returns & Dues

- **Replacements** — view replacement records (the menu appears when there are received
  returns at the office). See the mapping of each defective device to its replacement.
- **Pending Replacements** — see approved defects that are awaiting their replacement
  device, split into awaiting/ready/waiting-for-shipment counts.
- **Return Requests** — view the return records in your area. When a defect you reported
  is approved, a return request is created automatically for the defective device —
  send the device back so management can confirm it reached PDIC and process the
  replacement.
- **Pending Payments** — view the payment dues owed in your area (shown per user with
  the due amount for each received defective device).

## User management (scoped)

Grow and organize your team directly:

- **My Users** — see the clusters and operators under your sub-distribution. Use **Add
  User** to create new accounts in your team. The roles you can create are
  **Sub Distribution MD/Manager**, **Cluster**, **Operator**, and **Sub Distribution
  Employee**.
- **User Hierarchy** — view and navigate your team as a tree (clusters and their
  operators), and add users from the tree.
- **Bulk Upload** — upload a CSV of users for your area at once. As a Sub Distributor
  you can upload **Operators** (under your sub-distribution or a cluster under you) and
  **Clusters** (directly under you).

## Employee Activities

**Employee Activities** shows the timeline of meaningful actions performed by your
**sub-distribution employees** — so you can keep an eye on the activity in your branch
without leaving your own scope.

## Reports (scoped)

- **Cluster Report** — a scoped view of the clusters under your sub-distribution: each
  cluster's operators, device counts, and SB/ONT/Other split.
- **Operator Report** — a scoped view down to each operator under you: their digital /
  broadband IDs, parent cluster, and device counts.

Both report pages accept a **date range** and a **Filter Report** search, and let you
**Export PDF** or download the data.

## External Inventory

**External Inventory** shows the external stock catalog (items like OTT boxes, OLTs,
remotes, and adapters) with each item's name and price — useful to see what is available
in the catalog for your area.

## Features summary

"You can" — receive and confirm deliveries, distribute devices to your clusters and
operators, track any device, report defects and confirm replacement receipts, view
replacements, returns, and pending payments, create users for your team, bulk-upload
users, watch your employees' activities, and view scoped Cluster/Operator reports and
the external inventory catalog.

# MD / Director Guide

As **MD / Director** you have an executive oversight role. You can **view** the full
organisation — all users, devices, distributions, defects, reports, and the activity
audit — all in a **read-only** view. Your dashboard is
the **Admin Dashboard** — the executive view of the whole organisation.

Read **[getting-started.md](getting-started.md)** first for login and the shared interface.

## Your dashboard

Your landing page is the **Admin Dashboard**: executive KPIs, charts, alerts, reliability
metrics, and recent users and defects, with a date range filter.

## Users

- **All Users** — read-only list of every account. You can search, filter, and open a
  user's details.
- **User Hierarchy** — view the distribution structure tree (who reports to whom).

## Devices

- **All Devices** — read-only table of every device. Search by serial/type, view details
  and history.
- **Track Device** — enter a serial number to see its current **location and status chain**.
  Useful for executive audits.

## Distribution

- **All Distributions** — view every distribution record, its devices, and its status.
  Viewing only.

## Defects & Returns

- **Defect Reports** — view all reported defects and their status. Viewing only.
- **Returns** — view return records. Viewing only.

## Reports

You can open **every report**:

- **Reports & Analytics** — overview with filters and date range.
- **Sub Distribution Report** — sub-distribution level reporting.
- **Cluster Report** — cluster-level totals and search.
- **Operator Report** — operator-level reporting.

## System & Audit

- **Backups** — view available **database backup documents**. Downloading is permitted for
  the backup list.
- **Activities** — the full **audit activity log**, filterable by actor and date. This is
  your key tool for executive oversight of who did what.
- **View User Dashboard** — search for a user and **view their dashboard** as if you were
  them, to inspect how the system looks to that role.

## Features summary

"You can" — view all users, devices, distributions, defects, returns, every report,
backups, the activity audit, and view-as user dashboards. The system is **read-only** for
your role by design.

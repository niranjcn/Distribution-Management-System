# Cluster Guide

As **Cluster** you handle field distribution and support in your cluster: you create
distributions, confirm deliveries, report and manage defects, handle replacements,
returns, and pending payments, and you create/submit defect reports. Your dashboard is
the **Sub Distributor Dashboard** (scoped to your cluster).

Read **[getting-started.md](getting-started.md)** first for login and the shared interface.

## Your dashboard

The scoped dashboard shows your cluster's KPIs: devices, distributions, return requests,
and defects.

## Devices

- **My Devices** — the devices in your cluster. View details and history.
- **Track Device** — view a device's location and status chain by serial.

## Distribution

- **Distribution** — create distributions to hand devices to operators under your cluster.
- **Scoped Distributions** — view distributions in your cluster and their status.
- **Delivery Confirmations** — confirm receipt of distributions delivered to you.

## Defects

- **Defect Reports** — create a new defect report when you find a defective device; view
  and manage your cluster's reports. A defect report typically records which device is
  faulty, the issue, and photos.
- **To create a defect report:** open the defects page, choose **create**, search/select
  the defective device, describe the issue, attach a photo if available, and submit.

## Replacements, Returns & Dues

- **Replacements** — view replacement items (menu appears when there are received returns
  at the office) and manage the replacement workflow.
- **Replacement Confirmation** — confirm receipt of a replacement device when it arrives.
- **Returns** — submit return records for defective/excess devices and manage their status.
- **Pending Payments** — view payment dues in your cluster.

## User management (scoped)

- **All Users** — list users in your scope; you can create users in your team within your
  cluster.

## Reports (scoped)

- **Operator Report** — scoped reporting view for your operators.
- **Report** — your scoped reporting overview.

## External Inventory

- **External Inventory** — view external inventory items relevant to your cluster.

## Features summary

"You can" — create distributions and confirm deliveries, create/manage defect reports,
manage replacements and returns, view/manage pending payments, confirm replacement
receipt, manage your team's users (scoped), and view scoped reports and external inventory.

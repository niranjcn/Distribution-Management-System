# Sub Distribution Employee Guide

As **Sub Distribution Employee** you work in the field supporting your sub-distribution
team: you receive and confirm deliveries, manage replacement confirmation and returns,
report your own approval requests, and view your team's devices, distributions, defects,
and scoped reports. Your dashboard is the **Sub Distributor Dashboard**.

Read **[getting-started.md](getting-started.md)** first for login and the shared interface.

## Your dashboard

The scoped dashboard shows your team's KPIs: devices, distributions, return requests,
and defects.

## Devices

- **My Devices** — the devices handled in your team. View details and history.
- **Track Device** — view any device's location and status chain by serial.

## Distribution

- **Distribution** — create distributions as needed for your field work.
- **Scoped Distributions** — view your team's distributions and their status.
- **Delivery Confirmations** — confirm receipt of distributions delivered to you. Always
  confirm deliveries so the record stays accurate.

## Approvals — your key area

Unlike most field users, you **submit approval requests** rather than review them:

- **My Approval Requests** — the requests you have submitted and their status.
- To **submit an approval request:** from the relevant action (for example a field change
  or a request needing sign-off), submit the request. It enters the request workflow and
  appears here for you to track. Your manager (Sub Distribution MD/Manager or Sub
  Distributor) reviews it on the Sub Distribution / Operations Approvals pages and
  approves or rejects it. When approved, the requested action becomes available.

## Defects, Replacements, Returns & Dues

- **Defect Reports** — view and manage defect reports; create new reports for defective
  devices.
- **Replacements** — view replacement items (shown when there are received returns at the
  office).
- **Replacement Confirmation** — confirm receipt of a replacement device when it arrives.
- **Returns** — submit return records and manage their status.
- **Pending Payments** — view payment dues for your team.

## User management (scoped)

- **All Users** — view the users in your team hierarchy (read-only for your level).

## Reports (scoped)

- **Cluster Report** and **Operator Report** — scoped reporting views.
- **Report** — your scoped reporting overview.

## External Inventory

- **External Inventory** — view external inventory items relevant to you.

## Features summary

"You can" — create distributions and confirm deliveries, manage/report defects and dead
returns, confirm replacement receipt, **submit and track your approval requests**, view
your team's users, and view scoped reports and external inventory.

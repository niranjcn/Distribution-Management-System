# Getting Started

This section applies to **every user role**. Read it once — login, security, and the
common interface are the same for everyone. Then open the guide for your specific role.

## Signing in

1. Open the DMS website in your browser.
2. Click **Login** (or open the login page directly).
3. Enter either your **registered email address** or **phone number**, and your **password**.
4. Optionally tick **Remember Me** so the site keeps you signed in on this browser.
5. Click the login button.

When the login succeeds you are taken to your personal dashboard. The menu you see will be
filtered to your role — you only ever see the pages your role is allowed to use.

### Troubleshooting login

- **Wrong password** — double-check caps lock. After five failed attempts in a short window,
  the account is locked for a system security period; wait and try again.
- **Forgot password** — click the **Forgot password** link on the login page. A message tells
  you to contact PDIC, who will reset your credentials for you.
- **You are forced to update details on login** — the first time you log in (or when an administrator
  marks your account for update), you are redirected to a security page and must set a new email
  and/or a new password before you can continue. Do this once and then enter the site.

## The main interface

After login you see the application shell, which is the same for every role:

- **Sidebar (left)** — your role-filtered menu. Click a section to expand it and see its pages.
- **Top bar** — shows your current location (breadcrumbs), a **notification bell**, a
  **dark / light theme** toggle, and your **profile menu** at the far right.
- **Main area** — the content of the current page.
- The open site will sign you out automatically after a period of inactivity for security. Just
  log back in to continue.

### Notifications

The bell in the top bar shows you **how many unread alerts** you have (a red badge). Click it to
open the **Notifications** page, which has two tabs:

- **Activities / Alerts** — system events related to you (device movements, status changes).
- **Requests / Approvals** — items sent to you for action or that you have submitted.

Use this page to stay on top of things that need your attention.

### Your profile and logging out

Click your **profile menu** (top right):

- **Profile** — view and edit your own details.
- **Logout** — end your session. Timing out or logging out protects the account.

## Navigation tips

- The **breadcrumbs** at the top tell you where you are (for example
  `Home / Devices / All Devices`).
- Several menu sections are **collapsible** — click the section name to open or close the sub-menu.
